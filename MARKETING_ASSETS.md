## Sales Scripts

### Demo Call Script (20 minutes)
**Min 0-2: Discovery**
"Before I show you anything, I want to make sure this is a fit. How many leads do you get per month roughly? And what's your current follow-up process?"

**Min 2-5: Pain identification**
"How much time do you spend manually following up with leads each week? And how often do you lose jobs because you couldn't get back to someone fast enough?"

**Min 5-15: Product demo**
- Show the chat widget capturing a lead live
- Show AI qualification in action
- Show the automation sequences
- Show the dashboard/CRM

**Min 15-18: ROI math**
"If you're getting 40 leads/month and currently closing 18%, that's about 7 jobs. Our customers typically see that jump to 28-35%. At your average job value of [$X], that's [X] additional jobs × [$X] = [$X]/month in new revenue. LeadFlow is $149/month. ROI pays back in the first extra job."

**Min 18-20: Close**
"Given what you've seen today, does this make sense for your business? The 14-day trial is free, no card. I can get you set up right now in about 20 minutes."

### Objection Handling

**"I'm too busy"**
→ "That's exactly why you need this. LeadFlow saves our average customer 12 hours per week on follow-ups. The setup takes 20 minutes and I'll do it with you on this call."

**"I already have a system"**
→ "What does your current system do when a lead comes in at 2am? LeadFlow handles that automatically. Most businesses we talk to have a CRM but no automation — that's the gap we fill."

**"It's too expensive"**
→ "At $149/month, you need one extra job per month to break even. Given that you're getting [X] leads/month, adding 1 job from better follow-up is very realistic. What's your average job value?"

**"I'll think about it"**
→ "I totally understand. What's the one thing that would make this a clear yes? [Address it]. In the meantime, let me start your free trial so you can see the data — no card required, you can cancel anytime."
